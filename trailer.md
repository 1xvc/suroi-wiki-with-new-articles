# Trailer  
The **Trailer** is an indestructible obstacle added in the [v0.24.0](https://github.com/HasangerGames/suroi/releases/tag/v0.24.0) “Making Waves” update on October 22, 2023. It serves as a piece of cover, in the [Port](/buildings/port).

# Location & Spawning  
Trailers currently spawn only in the [Port](/locations/port). Only 2 Trailers spawn in the [Port](/buildings/port).

# Loot & More  
The Trailer cannot be destroyed and therefore drops no loot. It also cannot be interacted with or entered.

# Tips  
- Use Trailers for solid cover, as they are indestructible.  
- If you and your opponent are fighting in the middle of both Trailers, you can ricochet bullets off the other Trailer to hit the enemy.

# Trivia  
- The Trailer is likely a flatbed, with nothing being hauled in it.
